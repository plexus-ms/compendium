This section holds documentation about Plexus itself:

- [manifesto.md](manifesto.md) — **The Plexus Manifesto**: the high-level why. Readers unfamiliar with Plexus start here.
- [standard.md](standard.md) — **PLX, the Plexus Standard**: the map of concerns, one section each, pointing at the modules that cover it and naming what is not covered yet.
  Its § 1 holds the vocabulary, the module and maturity model, the declaration marker, versioning and the train, the upstream guarantees, governance, and the compendium's own conventions.
- [modules/](modules/) — **The modules**: one page per concern a consumer adopts on its own.
  Contracts and approaches at standard maturity carry BCP 14 requirements, cited by section and module code (e.g. "§ 2 PLX-App"); approaches at approach maturity and notes carry prose only.
- [reference-stack.md](reference-stack.md) — **The reference stack**: the module composition each train is tested against.
- [brand/](brand/) — **The Plexus Brand**: the visual identity.
