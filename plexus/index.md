This section holds the essential documentation about Plexus itself:

- [**The Plexus Manifesto**](manifesto.md) — Answers the high-level why. Readers unfamiliar with Plexus are recommended to start here.
- [**The Plexus Standard (PLX)**](standard.md) — the map of concerns, one section each, pointing at the modules that cover it and naming what is not covered yet.
  Its § 1 holds the vocabulary, the module and maturity model, the declaration marker, versioning and the train, the upstream guarantees, governance, and the compendium's own conventions.
  The modules it points at have a section of their own: [PLX Modules](../modules/index.md).
- [**The reference stack**](reference-stack.md) — the module composition each train is tested against.
- [**The Plexus Brand**](brand/index.md) — the visual identity.
